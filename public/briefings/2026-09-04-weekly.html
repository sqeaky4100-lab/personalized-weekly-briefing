OpenAINew · Sep 3Limited rollout

GPT-6 Astra aims at finished work, not just stronger answers

OpenAI says Astra improves coding, research, computer use, and complex multi-step work, including producing documents, spreadsheets, and presentations that follow supplied templates. Access began with a limited set of organizations on September 3; Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and API availability is planned over the coming days, so it may not yet appear in your model picker.

What changed
Astra is framed as an end-to-end work model: it can adapt when requirements change and carry a task from research through a formatted deliverable. The useful caveat is availability—this is a staged release, not universal access today.
Why this matters to you
Your strongest test is a complete audit loop, such as reconciling a TB to support and returning a review-ready workbook in your established format.
Try this
Using the attached TB, GL, and prior-year workpaper, build the requested audit schedule in the supplied template. Preserve formulas, identify every exception, independently recompute totals, and finish with a Purpose / Scope / Procedures / Conclusion memo. Do not conclude until all source-to-output ties are documented.

ChatGPT WorkNew · Sep 3Plan-limited

ChatGPT Work adds governed plugins and fresher workspace memory

OpenAI added beta Zendesk and OneNote plugins for eligible workspaces, along with centralized plugin permissions. Enterprise and Edu are also receiving improved memory that can keep relevant context current across chats; Enterprise begins with roughly two weeks of admin-controlled early access before default enablement for eligible workspaces.

What changed
Admins can search, install, enable, or restrict plugins by role from one settings surface, while users connect their own authorized accounts. Improved memory includes a reviewable summary, and admins or users can revert to legacy saved memories.
Why this matters to you
This is the governance layer your Kayenta and other audit projects need before cross-chat memory or Office content becomes dependable workpaper context.
Try this
If your work workspace receives improved memory, review its memory summary before the next Kayenta status run. Remove stale engagement facts, then compare the run’s conclusions with the authoritative tracker and source files—memory should guide retrieval, never replace evidence.

GoogleRolling out · Sep 3iOS

Gmail Live turns inbox search into a voice conversation

Google is rolling out Gmail Live globally in English on iOS and Android, letting you ask follow-up questions about inbox details and see the supporting emails in the transcript. Gmail Live requires Google AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra; Docs Live is iOS/Android for Pro and Ultra, while business Workspace support is still “coming soon.”

What changed
The features were previewed in May and began general mobile rollout on September 3. Gmail Live retrieves and cites inbox information; Docs Live can structure spoken ideas and, with permission, pull context from Gmail, Drive, Chat, and the web.
Why this matters to you
On your iPhone, this could become the fastest way to extract a viewing time or lender detail from Kathy’s thread while keeping the original email one tap away.
Try this
Open Gmail on iPhone and ask: “From Kathy’s latest property-viewing thread, list only confirmed addresses, viewing times, access instructions, and unresolved financing restrictions. Cite the email for every item.” Verify each cited message before using it.

PerplexityNew · Sep 2026Cloud API

A $0.15-per-million-token research sidecar is now realistic

Perplexity’s Agent and Router APIs added GLM 5.3 Flash at $0.15 per million uncached input tokens, $0.03 cached input, and $0.50 output. It is not a free tier, but the published rates make it practical to use for cheap preprocessing—classifying listings, compacting state, or normalizing OCR text—before sending only the difficult exceptions to a frontier model.

What changed
The new router model joins automatic prompt-cache keys for presets, which Perplexity says can reduce recurring preset costs by about 5% depending on cache reuse. Availability is through the cloud-hosted API and requires a Perplexity API account.
Why this matters to you
Your property and audit automations carry large repeated instructions; a cheap normalization pass plus a tiny exception packet can reduce both token use and context clutter.
Try this
Have the low-cost model convert each new listing into one fixed JSON schema and compare it with your Google Sheet state. Send the frontier model only changed fields, conflicts, and missing high-risk facts; retain the original source URL and agent value (chatgpt or KDT) unchanged.

World snapshotPublished · Sep 4

Global food prices reach their highest level since late 2022

The UN FAO Food Price Index rose 1.9% in August to 133.3, with every major commodity group higher; it was 2.5% above a year earlier but still 16.8% below the March 2022 peak. Sugar jumped 11.9%, while cereal prices rose amid weather concerns, disrupted Black Sea logistics, and input pressure linked to the Strait of Hormuz.

What changed
The September 4 release shows price pressure broadening across cereals, oils, meat, dairy, and sugar rather than staying isolated. FAO also highlighted lower crop prospects in parts of Europe and the United States.
Why this matters to you
This is an early signal for household grocery costs and for audit clients whose budgets or operations are sensitive to food, fuel, or transportation inputs.
Try this
For any food-service or assistance-program client, compare year-to-date food and transport costs with budget and prior year before planning analytics. Treat the FAO index as external context—not direct audit evidence for a recorded balance.
