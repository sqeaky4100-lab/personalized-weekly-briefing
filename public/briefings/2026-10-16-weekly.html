05 · WorldIMF outlookOct 13

AI investment props up growth while energy and debt raise the floor on risk

The IMF’s October outlook projects 3.1% global growth in 2026 and 3.4% in 2027, with AI investment and trade supporting activity. It simultaneously warns that higher energy and food prices, conflict, debt, and an uneven distribution of gains leave the outlook tilted to the downside.

The IMF says global resilience is increasingly uneven: countries tied to the AI-investment boom are benefiting while energy importers and lower-income economies face greater pressure. Its near-term inflation forecast reaches 4.7% globally, largely from energy and food.

For planning and review, the combination of higher-for-longer rates, energy volatility, and uneven demand is more useful than the headline growth number alone.

Add a concise macro-watch section to this planning memo. Map higher energy costs, higher-for-longer rates, and uneven demand to the client’s debt service, liquidity, estimates, impairment, and going-concern assumptions. Define the evidence or threshold that would trigger an update. Keep this separate from management’s assertions.
