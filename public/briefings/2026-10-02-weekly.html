ChatGPT’s iOS camera now turns a multi-page capture into one PDF

The iOS camera’s new Scan option captures several pages in sequence and automatically combines them into a single PDF. It is a small feature with immediate value for bank statements, signed support, invoices, and field documents that otherwise arrive as scattered photos.

Open the camera button in ChatGPT for iOS and choose Scan; multiple captured pages are combined into one PDF. Availability: OpenAI says the feature is rolling out on iOS, so it may not appear for every account immediately.

You can consolidate a bank-statement or PBC packet at the point of capture, reducing page-order errors and giving the review workflow one stable attachment instead of a chain of images.

For the next non-sensitive test packet, scan every page in order, confirm the page count and date range before upload, then ask: “Create a page-indexed table of statement dates, ending balances, missing pages, and unusual gaps. Cite the PDF page for every finding.” Follow your firm’s approved-data policy.

G7 moves to release 100 million barrels as diesel prices stay under pressure

G7 members agreed to coordinate the release of 100 million barrels of diesel and other fuel reserves through the International Energy Agency over four months, with a substantial diesel share front-loaded into the first 20 days. Markets reacted, but the release is a supply cushion—not proof that fuel costs have permanently normalized.

The coordinated reserve action is intended to ease tight fuel markets quickly, especially diesel. AP reported U.S. diesel at $6.37 per gallon on October 2 after a September 22 record of $6.52; oil prices fell on reserve-release discussions, but the outlook remains volatile.

Fuel volatility touches client-site travel, shipping, and property operating assumptions—so near-term relief should not be treated as a permanent baseline.

For travel or property comparisons this month, show fuel-sensitive costs separately and include a simple ±10% sensitivity. Refresh actual quotes before a decision rather than using today’s price as a long-term assumption.
